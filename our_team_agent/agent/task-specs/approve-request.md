# T04 Approve Request

**Automation level:** L0 (manual, human decision)

**Purpose:** The faculty advisor makes the final approve or deny decision.

**Inputs and sources:** Review summary from T03 and the original request record.

**Outputs and recipients:** Decision (approve, deny, or need more info) and reasons, passed to T05.

**Tool:** None (manual task).

**Responsible person:** Faculty advisor.

**Response deadline:** 2 business days. After 1 day send a reminder; after 2 days escalate to the department chair.

**Limits:** Automated retries: not applicable.

**Success check:** A decision and reason are recorded for the request.
